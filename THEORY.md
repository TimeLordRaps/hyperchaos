# Hyperchaos: a field of diverging divergences

## What the field studies

**[HYPER] USER-STATED.** Hyperchaos concerns chaos of chaotic divergences:
what divergence means, how divergences diverge, and convergence toward
divergence. Changing an optimum on an otherwise orderly trajectory does not
establish this higher object. Divergences resolving toward convergent states
instead point toward Hyperorder.

**[FRAME] Research program.** Ordinary divergence studies alternative
continuations of a process. Hyperchaos changes the object from one resulting
route to the continuation processes that themselves generate further routes.
It asks whether alternatives merely end differently or each open further
choices; whether reconvergence resolves a family or lands in another
divergence-producing region; and what survives a change in observation.

This repository supplies a finite theory and an executable witness mechanism
for those questions. It is an operational research beginning. Native closure
is not established by a repository name, diagram or Python test.

## Native ancestry and exact transfer gaps

**[HYPER] Sourced vocabulary.** Public Hypermath separates ground, relations,
operations and ordinatics. `□` is ground/application; `~~` is continuation
overlap; `=~` is same substance regardless of path; `==` is mutual simulation.
The L1 concrete implication subpath is `== ⇒ =~ ⇒ ~~`. It remains part of
the source structure, but is not the complete filtration. The published
quadrilateral architectural specification adds `~=` as transitive abstraction,
distinct from `=~` substance:

- The architecture has an off-branch from `~~` to `~=`.
- It describes a retrace from abstraction toward mutual simulation only
  with a substance-realizability condition.
- It does not define a linear chain of four relations or identify the two
  differently oriented symbols `~=` and `=~`.

**[OPEN] Source-level restriction.** The pinned public L2 `.hm` file does
not contain that abstraction branch. The public quadrilateral Lean file
does: its `Abstraction` is generated from `Similar` with reflexive, symmetric
and transitive constructors. Its `SubstanceWitness(x,y)` is already the
mutual-simulation proposition `(x ≡ y)`, and `conditionalRetrace` returns
that supplied witness. The displayed conditional conclusion is therefore
already supplied by its strong premise; this does not derive an independent realizability witness
from abstract pattern equivalence. The architecture's stronger equivalence
and grounding language is therefore not imported as a discharged theorem.
[PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md) pins these distinct public source surfaces.

**[FRAME]** The finite construction below uses state identifiers, supplied
choice equivalence classes, sets and a programming language. These are
observation infrastructure, not a derivation of new primitives from `□`.

| Native layer | Problem studied here | Transfer that remains [OPEN] |
|---|---|---|
| L0 ground | Origin of continuation alternatives | Derive each admitted act from `□`, rather than accept a transition declaration |
| L1 relations | Same-choice and same-matter presentations | Prove the supplied quotient respects the concrete `== ⇒ =~ ⇒ ~~` subpath |

Pattern equality below is neither native `~=` nor `==`. Settled matter
equality is not an established native `=~`. Overlap of finite terminal
capacities is not a complete witness of `~~`. The finite pattern operation
abstracts presentation detail, but a relation-preserving native mapping and
its conditional retrace are separate proof obligations. Those casts would
skip the bridge obligations.

## Finite definitions

All definitions and propositions in this section are **[FRAME]**.

Let `S` be a finite set of observed states. Let `E` contain continuation acts
`(s, c, t)` with source `s ∈ S`, target `t ∈ S` and optional choice class `c`.
A choice class declares a semantically distinct continuation act at a
source. Its spelling has no order, metric, probability or physical units.
Two presentations of one act belong to one class; adding a state identifier
does not create another semantic alternative. The caller supplies and must
justify this quotient.

The directed observation is acyclic. Every terminal declares settled matter
`m(s)`, which may agree across different terminal identifiers. Every maximal
route therefore ends at a declared boundary. These are input assumptions,
not assertions about all reality.

**First divergence.** A site has multiple outgoing semantic choice classes.
This is a structural opening into distinct continuation acts. It does not
assert physical sensitivity, moral harm, metric separation or loss of
capacity. Branch counts alone cannot authenticate the supplied semantic
distinctions; source evidence remains necessary in a real application.

**Settled capacity.** `C(s)` is the set of declared matter classes at all
terminals reachable from `s`. Here `C` is a finite observed capacity, not all
native continuation capacity. Reachability includes the starting state. If
`C(s)` has one matter class, the selected boundary is resolved, but the
lineage may still contain divergent processes.

**Separate branch regions.** For two choices from a site `s` landing at
`a` and `b`, let `R(a)` and `R(b)` be their reflexive reachable sets.
`J = R(a) ∩ R(b)` is their common region. Before reconvergence, the exclusive
regions are `R(a) \ J` and `R(b) \ J`. These sets have no physical units.

**Divergence of divergences.** A witness contains a site `s`, two distinct
choices, and two routes entirely inside their respective exclusive regions
to their first divergence sites. The alternatives themselves therefore open
further alternatives. They are not merely different settled outcomes.
Identical local split patterns still give two divergent processes;
non-isomorphism is not a requirement in Tyler's supplied definition.

**Convergence toward divergence.** A witness contains two distinct choices,
routes with disjoint interiors to their first shared state `j`, and an
onward route to a first divergence site. If `j` already diverges, that onward
route is empty. Reconvergence can unite the routes into a generator of new
alternatives, instead of resolving them into order.

**Pattern of divergence.** A terminal has pattern `settled`. A unary state
inherits its successor's pattern. A fork has `fork(M)`, where `M` is the
multiset of its successor patterns. This erases node names, choice spellings,
terminal matter spellings and branch ordering. It retains the multiplicity
of distinct semantic alternatives. Two binary forks with equal children
share a pattern; binary and ternary forks do not. A fork into two further
forks differs from a fork into settled trajectories.

Unequal patterns are a pattern-variation facet, not a further requirement
for Hyperchaos. The recursion can continue through any finite nesting within
the observation budget. Cached pattern classes prevent repeated expansion
of shared continuation substructures. Their equality remains an observation
of split structure, not full lineage or native simulation equality.

## Propositions and counterexamples

**P1 — Ordinary redirection does not establish the higher object.** A root
has two choices, each leading only through unary continuation to an optimum.
The optima may differ. Neither exclusive region contains another divergence
site, so no divergence-of-divergences witness exists. This is Tyler's
butterfly-redirection control.

**P2 — Self-similar splitting can establish the higher object.** A root
forks into `A` and `B`; each separately forks into two terminals. Both are
divergence sites before any join, so the root has an iterated witness even
though their recursive patterns agree. Requiring different shapes would
exclude this case for a reason absent from the supplied definition.

**P3 — Renaming and unary insertion preserve observation.** A bijection of
state identifiers preserves incidence, common regions and lineage. A
bijection of choice names preserves their distinctness; one of matter names
preserves agreement. Unary insertion adds no fork and erases under pattern
recursion. Witness paths gain presentation edges but retain semantic acts.
Contraction must retain distinct choices: coalescing two acts into one graph
edge changes the supplied semantics rather than preserving presentation.

**P4 — Common settled matter cannot erase local higher divergence.** Attach
routes from all leaves of P2 to a common settled state. `A` and `B` still
diverge in separate regions before that boundary; the local iterated witness
remains. Yet the terminal capacity contains one matter class. The report
therefore gives the selected boundary Hyperorder direction while preserving
the local Hyperchaos motif. A terminal-only projection would lose the latter.

**P5 — A shared divergence is not two independent divergences.** Two routes
can meet at one fork. That fork is in their common region, so it cannot
witness both sides' exclusive divergence. The correct local observation is
convergence toward divergence. One shared generator is not double-counted.

**P6 — Pattern equality is weaker than lineage equality.** A fork whose
routes merge at one terminal and a fork whose routes end at two different
terminal presentations both have `fork({settled: 2})`. Only the former has
a shared-state join. Equal patterns therefore establish neither equal join
structure nor native `==`. Singleton settled capacity also does not imply
identical paths.

These propositions follow from the finite definitions. The suite tests
examples and counterexamples. An independent oracle enumerates complete
paths for all 1,024 acyclic five-state structures and compares terminal
capacity and iterated witnesses with the graph-search implementation.
This is a bounded adversarial sweep, not an unbounded correctness proof.

## Field relationships

**[HYPER]** Hyperorder owns its sourced closure/dichrome field. This field
uses Tyler's new directional relationship without replacing that sense.
Hyperemergence owns newly emerging coherence. Diverging regimes might
produce an emergence candidate, but branching structure alone does not
establish coherence. These are conceptual relationships, not validated
runtime dependencies.

Hyperethics owns normative analysis. A divergence is not automatically
malicious. Necessity, causation, intent, authority and standing require
separate evidence; this mechanism produces no ethical verdict. The earlier
question about an ethical ground remains open.

## Research frontier

Each next extension is **[OPEN]**, with a refutable gate:

1. Build a sourced relation-preserving map from native derivations to choice
   and matter declarations. Test equal and unequal native presentations
   before claiming a `~~`, `=~`, `~=` or `==` bridge. Keep the concrete and
   abstraction routes distinct, and do not treat an already-assumed
   mutual-simulation witness as independently constructed retrace evidence.
2. Handle recurrent continuation without equating a cycle with nonclosure.
   A native or coinductive construction must preserve return and aperture;
   a clock cutoff cannot be substituted for that argument.
3. Generalize beyond the two exclusive branch regions. The current witness
   suffices for one finite motif; it does not define away other ways
   divergences overlap, transform, depend on context or change direction.
4. Qualify projection between observation boundaries. P6 supplies an exact
   loss-of-information counterexample. Two reports with different boundaries
   do not automatically concern the same proposition.
5. Study an actual branching system using independently justified choice
   classes and measured behavior. The current demonstrations are finite
   structural examples, not empirical qualification.

The present artifact is a field with a supplied sense, explicit theory,
inspectable witnesses, runnable operations and refutable limits. Native
closure and real-system qualification remain separate gates.
