## The dynamics meaning

EXTERNAL. O. E. Rössler, "An equation for hyperchaos", *Physics Letters A*
71 (1979) 155–157,
[doi:10.1016/0375-9601(79)90150-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/0375-9601(79)90150-6).
In the dynamics literature that followed, hyperchaos is chaos with at least
two positive Lyapunov exponents. It is recorded as the
`two-positive-lyapunov-exponents` usage, so that no ruling takes it on
without saying so.
